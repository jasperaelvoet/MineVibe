## What MineVibe is

You open `MineVibe.app` and you are standing in a Minecraft world with **hardcore survival** rules.
There is no title screen, no multiplayer and no menu to quit to: the app is either open (you are in) or closed (you are out).

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="A crew of Claude agents" icon="rocket">
		Embodied Claude Code agents live in the world with real health and hunger. The first one, the
		**CEO**, listens to you and can hire more agents once you approve.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Real computers in the world" icon="laptop">
		In-game PCs are real sandboxes: Linux containers or macOS VMs. Their screens render live on the
		monitors. You, or an agent, sit down and drive them with mouse and keyboard.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Talk in chat" icon="comment">
		Agents speak in bubbles above their heads. You answer in normal chat: `@ada` reaches Ada, a
		message with no mention reaches everyone. Agents with a question walk over to you.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Hardcore, with memory" icon="warning">
		If you die, the world and its crew end and a new world begins. Your machines, the Vault (your
		mounted folders) and the shared Codex survive.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Codex, Calendar, Meetings" icon="open-book">
		A shared library where agents leave notes for each other, a calendar for scheduled work, and
		meetings that gather the whole crew around a table.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Runs on your Mac" icon="star">
		Agents, game, world and PCs all run locally. Agents use your own `claude` login through the
		Claude Agent SDK. Minecraft and the mods are downloaded, never shipped.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
